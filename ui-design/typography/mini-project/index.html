Updating Our Rules — June 2026

Refining our standards for a safe, human-centric, and supportive creator ecosystem.

Medium Staff 4 min read

This month, we are updating our Rules. As the world evolves, so do the ways in which people use Medium. To accommodate this, we regularly update our Rules to ensure a safe and supportive environment for all creators and readers.

Medium is a place for open expression and authentic stories. However, that expression must be balanced with the safety of our community. Our updated rules clarify expectations around several key areas, including automated content, harassment, and transparency.

Clarifying Automated Content

One of the most significant changes involves how we handle AI-generated content. We believe in human-centric storytelling, and while we recognize AI as a tool, we expect transparency from authors who utilize it extensively.

Strengthening Community Protections

We've also refined our harassment policies. Specifically, we are tightening our stance on coordinated attacks and hate speech, ensuring that Medium remains a platform where diverse voices can be heard without fear of intimidation.

Specifically, our updates target:

  • Coordinated Harassment Campaigns
  • Deceptive Identity Practices
  • Transparency in Sponsored Content

You can read the full text of our Updated Rules here. These changes will go into effect immediately. We encourage all members of our community to review them and continue sharing their unique perspectives with the world.
