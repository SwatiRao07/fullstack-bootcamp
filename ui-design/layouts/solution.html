Drill 4 — Article Layout (Medium-style)

The Art of Simple Layouts

Creating readable content is the cornerstone of web design. By limiting line lengths to between 60–75 characters, we ensure the reader doesn't lose their place while scanning from the end of one line to the start of the next.

This layout uses a max-width of 70ch (characters) and center alignment, providing a distraction-free reading experience that mirrors high-quality editorial content.

Optimal spacing between paragraphs (vertical rhythm) further enhances the reading experience, allowing the eye to rest and process information in digestible chunks.
