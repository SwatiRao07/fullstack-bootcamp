Medium: The Diversity Report

A look into our progress and goals toward building a more inclusive company.

By Medium Team · July 2025

Diversity, equity, and inclusion are at the heart of how we grow our company and our community. This report shares our journey, our challenges, and the steps we are taking toward a more inclusive workplace.

Over the past year, we’ve made measurable progress. Representation of women and underrepresented groups has increased across engineering, design, and leadership. But there’s still a long way to go.

What We’ve Learned

  • Data transparency drives accountability.
  • Inclusive hiring requires constant iteration.
  • Cultural change begins with empathy and education.
“We believe that building a truly inclusive culture means listening, learning, and acting together.”

Next Steps

We’re expanding our partnerships with organizations supporting underrepresented talent in tech. We’re also strengthening mentorship programs and internal inclusion training.
